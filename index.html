About Me...

I'm a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Florida, where I work with Dr. Arkaprava Roy to develop statistical methods and machine-learning approaches for complex biomedical data. I also work part-time with Dr. Seth Munholland, Lead Technologist at the International Society for Computational Biology, on web development and descriptive statistical illustration.

I've always been curious about the fundamental of math and technology. Understanding a system often leads me to question how it could be improved. I frequently find myself asking, "Why doesn't it do this instead?" or "Why does it work here and not there?" What began as an interest in understanding the foundation of simple tools and equations developed into a passion for programming, mathematics, and statistical methodology. I also found out that understanding something on a fundamental level helped tremendously with explaining it to others.

Some of my earliest experiences with programming and collaboration came through online gaming communities. I enjoyed developing third-party tools that streamlined repetitive activities or made them more fun, and improved the experience for myself and others. Seeing friends use and appreciate something I had created was incredibly rewarding. Equally important were the friendships, shared problem-solving, and opportunities to help others along the way. Those experiences shaped my appreciation for mentorship and teamwork, and taught me that building something useful is often as rewarding as the process of building it together.

Today, that same curiosity and collaborative mindset continue to shape my work. Whether I'm developing statistical methodology, writing software, or working with others to solve challenging problems, I'm motivated by the opportunity to understand something deeply, create something useful, and contribute to the people and communities around me.
