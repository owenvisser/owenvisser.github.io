About Me...

I'm a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Florida, where I work with Dr. Arkaprava Roy to develop statistical methods and machine-learning approaches for complex biomedical data. I also work part-time with Dr. Seth Munholland, Lead Technologist at the International Society for Computational Biology (ISCB), on web development and descriptive statistical illustration.

I've always been curious about the foundations of mathematics and technology, particularly understanding how things work and how they could be improved. This curiosity initially drew me toward programming, where I enjoyed developing tools for online gaming communities that simplified repetitive tasks and helped others. These early experiences shaped my interests in statistical methodology, software development, and collaborative problem-solving, while also teaching me the importance of mentorship and communicating complex ideas clearly.

Today, I bring that same curiosity and collaborative mindset to my research and professional work. Whether developing statistical methodology, writing software, or working with others, I'm motivated by the opportunity to alleviate challenges and contribute to the broader research community.
